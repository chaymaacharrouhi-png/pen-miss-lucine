Miss Lucine writes rich, atmospheric fiction for readers who appreciate craft, mood, and enduring characters.

Atmospheric worlds

Memorable characters

Complex, elegant protagonists anchor each work with emotion and subtle depth.

Deliberate pacing

Slow-burning narratives and sensory detail give each book a luxurious pace.
